# Cookies and Privacy

Last updated 20th August 2026.

## Privacy statement

Please [see our privacy statement](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/data-protection/privacy-notice/embl-ebi-public-website/) on the usage of your data to deliver this resource.

## Cookies statement

We use essential cookies needed for the site to work.

When analytics is enabled on the data portal, we also use **non-essential** cookies for [Matomo](https://matomo.org/) so we can understand how the service is used and improve it. No data is shared with third parties for advertising.

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies via the banner on the data portal. Your choice is stored in a `cookies-accepted` cookie for one year. Matomo tracking only runs after you accept.
